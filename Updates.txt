  JaaGee Brand Language

  QUIET.
  ELEGANT.
  COMFORTABLE.
  PROFESSIONAL.
  TRUSTWORTHY.

  JaaGee should communicate confidence through restraint.

  The website must not feel loud, promotional, generic,
  AI-generated, or unnecessarily complicated.

  Visual decisions should prioritize:
  - whitespace
  - typography
  - photography
  - hierarchy
  - factual information
  - technical precision
  - comfortable navigation
  - restrained use of colour

  Copy should be:
  - factual
  - calm
  - concise
  - knowledgeable
  - human
  - technically accurate

  Avoid:
  - exaggerated marketing claims
  - unnecessary superlatives
  - excessive animation
  - visual clutter
  - generic corporate language
  - fabricated statistics
  - fabricated testimonials
  - invented technical claims
  - aggressive sales language

  Trust should be demonstrated through evidence,
  not claimed through adjectives.
